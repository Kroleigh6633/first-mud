===== nude-character =====
=== playbook-runner: nude-character — Nude character viability ===
Player only (no companions, no imbues, no gear proxy). Where does a bare character alone stop working? DESIGN INTENT (creative pass #7): at d=0 a timber-wolf is unscaled (hp 28) so any player >=1 should trivialise it in <3 rounds. A bare character 1v1 vs a single monster is a FAVORABLE matchup regardless of danger because the monster is alone and has no ally piling on. Where this playbook earns its keep is in the 'character outgrows gear-danger gap' cliff: pl-5 hits balance at d~4, hard at d~6, punishing at d~8. Higher-level bare characters keep clearing because 1v1 scaled-monster vs pl-12 strike-base favours the caster. This is acceptable: overworld threat comes from PACKS, not solo mobs (see `full-party` + future `pack` playbook).
