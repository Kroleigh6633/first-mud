# Aeldran Zones — Starting Road Outward

Zones are ordered by narrative proximity to the player's first hour. Each has:
- **Vibe** (atmospheric prose)
- **Landmarks** (named, hookable)
- **Faction presence**
- **Resource / encounter mix**
- **Hook** (one sentence to pull a player in)
- **Evolved state** (what has changed when the player returns post-Trusted)

Flag: region names and macro-geography are canon. Zone-scale names below (hamlets, specific ruins, minor roads) are **additions** — approve or rename.

---

## 1. The Starting Road — Eastmile Stretch

**Vibe.** Wet gravel and yellow gorse. The King's Road here runs between low stone walls that have outlived three kings. Crows on the walls. Mist in the dawn, flies by noon. It is the kind of road that looks safe exactly long enough to stop being safe.

**Landmarks.**
- **The Milepost at Eastmile** — a leaning Ardweld waystone, still humming faintly. The player's brooch reacts to it on first pass.
- **The Bent Mare Inn** — coach stop, Rider Post relay. Innkeeper: Senna Orrick (widow, runs a clean house, knows who owes whom).
- **The Crow-Gibbet** — an old execution frame. Rope has rotted; the beam remains. Caervorn-era deterrent.
- **Grieve's Ford** — shallow crossing. Bandit ambush site, historically.

**Factions.** Nominal Caervorn jurisdiction (light presence). Compact couriers use the road freely. Gravenguard scouts pass through quietly.

**Resources / encounters.** Common herbs (ribwort, blue-moss), timber scraps, iron nails salvaged from old wagon wrecks. Encounters: road bandits (low-tier humans), carrion hounds, a lone Caervorn patrol that wants to see your brooch.

**Hook.** The delivery the player is carrying was never meant to arrive — and someone on this road already knows that.

**Evolved state (post-Trusted).** The gibbet has a fresh body. Senna is serving twice as many drinks and sleeping half as much. A named courier from Portmere has taken a room indefinitely. The brooch hums louder at the Milepost now.

## 2. Eastmile Village

**Vibe.** Twenty-one houses, a forge, a chapel to nobody in particular, and more sheep than people. Slate roofs, peat smoke, a brook that freezes solid in winter. The kind of place where a stranger is discussed for a week.

**Landmarks.**
- **Thaddick's Forge** — run by Thaddick Orrin (deaf in one ear from hammering, honest prices, hates Caervorn tax collectors with a specific quiet passion).
- **The Chapel of Un-Named Saints** — caretaker Brother Velm; locals leave offerings to "whoever's listening." Brother Velm is an ex-Thornwood acolyte who left under a cloud.
- **The Old Oak** — village meeting tree. A coin jammed into the bark means a private favor is being asked.
- **Widow Moss's Cottage** — sells herbs, knows everyone's bowel complaints, absolutely a Thornwood informant.

**Factions.** Caervorn (tax only). Thornwood (quiet, through Widow Moss). Compact (monthly peddler). No faction *owns* Eastmile; that's part of why it matters.

**Resources / encounters.** Purchaseable: basic reagents, travel rations, salt, rope. Encounters: social only by default. Brawl possible in the common room on the wrong night.

**Hook.** Brother Velm recognizes the brooch. He has not told anyone he recognizes it.

**Evolved state (post-Trusted).** Widow Moss has either left or been arrested. A Caervorn garrison of six now billets at the Bent Mare — Thaddick's forge is commandeered two days a week. Brother Velm's chapel has been defaced with an Ashen sigil no one will admit to seeing.

## 3. The Thornwood Verge

**Vibe.** The forest begins not with a line but with a thickening. Birches go to oaks go to something older. Light arrives green. The Rootweave's edge makes compasses drift and makes the dead speak in dreams — though only to those with cause.

**Landmarks.**
- **The Gorsewitch Circle** — a ring of nine standing stones, one fallen. Coven meeting ground. Trespass is forgiven once.
- **Auld Maerwyn's Holt** — a hedge-witch's cottage that is further from the road each time the player visits (by design; the forest moves it).
- **The Hollow of the Gilded Stag** — a hunting blind used by Caervorn nobility in their grandfathers' time. Now mostly moss. An antler still hangs.
- **The Singing Brook** — its water, drunk in quantity, induces honest confession. Coven uses it at trials.

**Factions.** Thornwood Covens (openly). Caervorn patrols refuse to cross the verge; they stop at the Hollow. Wildfolk visible here to Aether-sensitive players.

**Resources / encounters.** Uncommon herbs (witchmace, silvercap mushroom), fae-touched timber, Wildfolk manifestation sites. Encounters: Thornwood wardens (initially Wary to non-mages), a Wyldeling (juvenile Wildfolk confused by intruders), poachers out of Caervorn territory.

**Hook.** A Thornwood apprentice is missing. Auld Maerwyn is not worried. The Eldest is — and won't say why.

**Evolved state (post-Trusted).** The fallen stone at Gorsewitch has been re-raised. A new circle has been seeded further in. Caervorn patrols are now *inside* the verge, nervously. The Singing Brook has gone silent in places — the Rootweave is retracting from stress.

## 4. Caervorn Marchkeep (Northern Spur)

**Vibe.** Grey walls, grey sky, grey uniforms. A garrison town on the highland foothills. Everything in Marchkeep is correct and nothing is kind. Bells ring on the hour, always on the hour, and the wrong kind of silence follows them.

**Landmarks.**
- **The Marchkeep Barracks** — Captain Bryn Hollemar commands; career officer, uneasy about recent Aldric decrees.
- **The Assay Office** — all magical items passing the region are logged, impounded, or destroyed. Assayer: Ewan Truce (pathologically thorough; secretly keeps one artifact for himself, and hates himself for it).
- **The Iron Gate** — checkpoint onto the Ironspire Road. Brooches are inspected. Lie well.
- **The Dry Well** — used for discreet executions. Officially dry since the last lord. Unofficially, not dry.

**Factions.** Caervorn (total). Gravenguard tolerated as professionals. Compact grudgingly permitted — *no* coven-marked goods, ever.

**Resources / encounters.** Iron ingots (high Workmanship, low magic), Caervorn-pattern arms (confiscated goods sold at auction), horse tack, suppressant herbs (anti-Weave reagents — rare, legal only here). Encounters: patrol inspections, duelist challenges (the garrison is bored), a single very intense Assessor visit if the player is tagged as magical.

**Hook.** Captain Hollemar needs a message carried *past* his chain of command — a Rider can do what an officer cannot.

**Evolved state (post-Trusted).** Marchkeep has doubled its garrison. The Dry Well is being filled with stone — someone wants it sealed before inspection. Hollemar is gone; a younger, louder captain, Vekter Stain, now commands. Ewan Truce has vanished *with* the artifact.

## 5. Gravenmarsh Causeway

**Vibe.** The road becomes raised stone across peat. Low fog never quite lifts. Water is everywhere and mostly black. Gravenhold sits on the horizon like a tombstone that was built with ambitions. The causeway is six miles long, and on foot it feels like sixty.

**Landmarks.**
- **The Sunken Wain** — a merchant cart half-swallowed by the bog, never recovered, never quite still either. Bodies inside. Visible at certain tides.
- **Reed-End Station** — Gravenguard waystation, manned at all hours by two scouts, currently Kesh and Orla (siblings, competent, bored enough to be chatty with a Rider).
- **The Asker's Stone** — an Ardweld monolith half-sunk. Stand on it and it asks you a question, once, and remembers your answer.
- **Gravenhold Outer Gate** — three portcullises in sequence. You can always enter. Leaving during lockdown is another matter.

**Factions.** Gravenguard (total, but formal rather than aggressive). Compact (via licensed caravan only). Golvari tolerated as specific named ambassadors.

**Resources / encounters.** Bog-iron, preservation-grade peat, uncommon reagents (marsh-candle lichen, sepulchre orchid), salvaged Ardweld fragments. Encounters: marsh-wights (Aether-undead, weak individually, swarm behavior), lost Gravenguard scout hallucinating from fever, smugglers using the bog to move unlicensed artifacts.

**Hook.** Commander Solan Drest has not held open audience in three weeks. The Asker's Stone, for the first time in recorded scout logs, has started asking the *same* question of everyone who stands on it.

**Evolved state (post-Trusted).** The Sunken Wain has been excavated — something was inside it that wasn't a body, and the site is now cordoned. Reed-End has been fortified. Orla is gone (transferred? dead? Kesh won't say). A second monolith has surfaced where none was before.

## Note on zone scaling

Each zone above is sized for roughly 3–8 hours of first-visit content. Zones 1–3 are first-act (Unknown → Known territory). Zone 4 is the first hard faction-pressure zone (Caervorn Wary punishes entry). Zone 5 is the on-ramp to Gravenguard storylines and the Ardweld Remnant chain.

Next pass should push outward to: the Thornwood deep (Rootweave zone), the first Compact City (Portmere), the Ashen Reach border, and the Drowned Coast entry.
