# Encounter Palette — By Zone / Biome

Archetypes, not stat blocks. Each entry: **who**, **why they're here** (in-world), **what they want**, **what they drop**. Designed to migrate directly into `content/monsters.json` entries once the stat schema is finalized.

Elemental alignments and polarity follow `lore/combat.md` and `lore/magic-system.md`.

---

## Starting Road / Eastmile Stretch (Common-tier)

### Road Bandit (Human, Mundane)
- **Who:** Displaced laborers, deserters, the occasional ex-soldier. Loosely organized; rarely named except for their leader.
- **Why here:** The Eastmile Stretch has low Caervorn patrol density and rich Compact courier traffic. Economics, not malice.
- **Wants:** Coin, trade goods, the horse if they can take it (they cannot — Rider horses cannot be lost permanently).
- **Drops:** Copper coin, knife (W2–W4 salvage), travel rations, occasionally a crumpled bounty notice on *another* bandit (quest hook).

### Carrion Hound (Beast, Neutral element)
- **Who:** Wild dog, starved, pack of 2–4.
- **Why here:** Follows the Crow-Gibbet scent trail. Eats what the road kills.
- **Wants:** Warm meat. Any warm meat.
- **Drops:** Hide (W1–W3), fang (basic reagent), occasionally a chewed leather pouch with contents intact.

### Caervorn Patrol (Human, Mundane; encounter varies by rep)
- **Who:** Four guards, one sergeant, on a regular loop.
- **Why here:** Showing the flag. Checking brooches. Bored.
- **Wants:** Compliance. Papers. To go back to Marchkeep before dark.
- **Drops:** (Only if combat — which costs rep.) Pattern arms (W5–W7), uniform tabards, sergeant's field-orders (intel item).

### Brook-Wisp (Wildfolk, Water, Shaping-aligned)
- **Who:** A juvenile Water Wildfolk resident in Grieve's Ford.
- **Why here:** The ford is a minor Weave node.
- **Wants:** Nothing. Watches. May manifest as fog or a ripple. Attacks only if repeatedly disrespected (drinking upstream of a body, for instance).
- **Drops:** Wildfolk essence (rare, only if the player is Aether-sensitive and the Wisp chooses).

## Eastmile Village & Surrounds (Common-tier, social-first)

### Village Drunk (Human, Mundane, non-hostile)
- **Who:** Cael Orrin, Thaddick's cousin. Drinks because his mother's farm went to a Compact debt call.
- **Why here:** He lives here. He is not an encounter so much as a *fixture* whose mood generates flavor events.
- **Wants:** Another drink. Someone to listen. Revenge he will never take.
- **Drops:** Rumor (free), small-favor quest hooks when sober.

### Wild Sheep-Worrier (Beast, Earth-aligned)
- **Who:** A large moor-dog gone feral, shaggy and scarred.
- **Why here:** Eastmile's flocks are undefended at dusk.
- **Wants:** Weak lambs. Will fight if cornered.
- **Drops:** Heavy hide (W3–W5), dense bone (Earth reagent).

## Thornwood Verge (Uncommon-tier, Wildfolk-active)

### Wyldeling (Wildfolk, random element, juvenile)
- **Who:** An unformed Wildfolk — not yet aligned cleanly. Curious, erratic, elemental leakage around it.
- **Why here:** Born from Rootweave surplus.
- **Wants:** To investigate strangers. Becomes hostile only if the player draws on Aether/Unmaking nearby.
- **Drops:** Wildfolk essence (if pacified and befriended — a mini-bond ritual, not loot).

### Thornwood Warden (Human, Mage, Shaping-aligned)
- **Who:** A coven-trained defender, paired with a Wildfolk.
- **Why here:** Patrolling the Verge for poachers and Caervorn incursion.
- **Wants:** To verify the player's business. Will escalate if the player has dead Wildfolk or Unmaking residue on them.
- **Drops:** (Combat only, and costs significant Thornwood rep.) Warden staff (W6–W8, Shaping-tapered), herb pouch, coven token (socially valuable).

### Boar-of-the-Deep-Brake (Beast, Earth)
- **Who:** A full-grown wild boar, territorial, tusks scarred from a past fight.
- **Why here:** The Verge has undergrowth no surface farmer reaches.
- **Wants:** To be left alone. Charges on aggro.
- **Drops:** Boar-tusk (W4–W7, weapon component), heavy hide, rich meat (rations).

### Fae-Touched Deer (Beast, Aether)
- **Who:** A stag with one wyrd-silver antler. Cannot be hunted conventionally.
- **Why here:** The Rootweave occasionally *marks* ordinary wildlife.
- **Wants:** To be seen. To lead the worthy somewhere. To flee from the unworthy.
- **Drops:** If killed: minor Wyrd-tangle penalty, an antler shard (Aether reagent, but cursed). If followed: a discovery site (quest hook, no combat).

## Caervorn Marchkeep (Uncommon-tier, social-heavy)

### Marchkeep Guard Patrol (Human, Mundane)
- **Who:** Three guards, one corporal, in-town patrol.
- **Why here:** Ordinary duty.
- **Wants:** Order, routine, no incident in their shift.
- **Drops:** Pattern arms (W4–W7), uniform, patrol schedule (intel).

### Duelist (Human, Mundane, aggressive on social trigger)
- **Who:** A bored off-duty officer, Lieutenant Vara Kest.
- **Why here:** Garrison life is tedious. She picks fights she's allowed to win.
- **Wants:** A clean duel, blood, a story to tell. *Will* respect a surrender.
- **Drops:** Duelist's blade (W7–W8), officer's sash (social-valuable in Caervorn territory, evidence elsewhere).

### Hidden Ashen Sympathizer (Human, Mundane + Aether-touched)
- **Who:** Ewan Truce, the Assayer (per threads). Only encounterable as combat if the player forces the issue.
- **Why here:** Canonical faction thread — Ashen Court asset inside Caervorn.
- **Wants:** To remain undetected. Will only fight cornered, and will Dream-Walk-flee if possible.
- **Drops:** (If defeated.) Assayer's ledger (major intel item), a refined dravenite chip (dangerous), an Ashen Court passphrase token.

## Gravenmarsh Causeway & Bog (Uncommon → Rare tier, Aether-active)

### Marsh-Wight (Undead, Aether/Unmaking, weak-swarm)
- **Who:** The restless dead of pre-Fractured-Age travelers. Not sapient. Not clean.
- **Why here:** The bog preserves bodies. Something in the Asker's Stone disturbs them periodically.
- **Wants:** Nothing coherent. Drains warmth. Drawn to light.
- **Drops:** Bog-iron fragment (W2–W4), a preserved personal effect (occasional unique — locket, wedding ring, folded letter with quest hook).

### Artifact Smuggler (Human, Mundane, possibly mage)
- **Who:** Two-to-four bog runners carrying unlicensed dravenite for an unnamed eastern buyer (Ashen Court).
- **Why here:** The bog hides the trail from Gravenguard scouts.
- **Wants:** To pass unseen. Will fight if confronted, surrender if outmatched.
- **Drops:** Raw dravenite (valuable, illegal to possess in Caervorn), a coded manifest, a map fragment of the smuggling route.

### Lost Scout (Human, Gravenguard, non-hostile by default)
- **Who:** A missing Gravenguard, fever-addled, half-drowned, alive.
- **Why here:** Got separated from patrol days ago.
- **Wants:** To be saved. Will reward the player's rep if returned alive.
- **Drops:** Scout's pack (rations, bandages, a scout-issue dagger W5), a sketched glyph the player might recognize (Chain 1 hook reinforcement).

### Bog-Drake (Beast, Water/Earth hybrid, rare encounter, mini-boss)
- **Who:** A serpentine bog predator, eight feet, shy except when territorial.
- **Why here:** Apex of the marsh food chain.
- **Wants:** Solitude and whatever dies in its water.
- **Drops:** Drake scale (W6–W8, rare armor component), drake venom (high-grade Unmaking-resistance reagent), drake eye (unique — Water Working focus, one per kill, does not respawn for many in-game days).

### Asker's Stone Manifestation (Ardweld, Aether, NOT combat — flag this)
- **Who:** The Stone speaks. Once per visit, it asks the question: *"Where is the one who names?"*
- **Why here:** Ardweld legacy, malfunctioning in a specific way tied to the Lost Expedition.
- **Wants:** An answer. It *remembers* each player's answer permanently.
- **Drops:** Not loot. A persistent world-state flag per player; certain later Gravenguard Honored quests key on which answer was given.

## Ardweld Ruin (Aeldran surface, Uncommon → Rare)

### Ward-Engine Sentinel (Construct, Neutral element, Shaping-aligned)
- **Who:** Ardweld automaton, function: perimeter defense. Still running after 1,200 years, mostly.
- **Why here:** Was placed here. Never told to stop.
- **Wants:** To exclude non-authorized presences. Recognizes certain Ardweld signifiers (the brooch) and may *not* attack if presented correctly.
- **Drops:** Resonance core fragment (rare automation component), dravenite dust, a functional ward-rune (Shaping taper precursor).

### Scavenger Band (Human, Mundane, hostile to all)
- **Who:** Grave-robbers who got to the ruin first. Loosely led by someone calling himself "Bren the Patient" (he is not).
- **Why here:** Ardweld salvage is worth more than any honest trade in the region.
- **Wants:** The player's gear, the player's life, and to finish looting before the Gravenguard arrives.
- **Drops:** Scavenger cache (rolled Ardweld fragments, mostly worthless, occasionally not), a map of three *other* scavenger sites.

### Echo-Thing (Aether, Unmaking-leaning, anomaly)
- **Who:** A partial remnant of something killed in the Sundering and not properly finished.
- **Why here:** Ruins are where the Sundering's unfinished work lives.
- **Wants:** Incomprehensible. Reacts to emotion more than to threat.
- **Drops:** Sundering shard fragment (rare, unstable), a single word in Ardweld that the player cannot translate yet.

## Thornwood Deep (Rare tier, Rootweave-active — staged for later zone pass)

### Thornwood Hunter-Pack (Human, Mage + Wildfolk, hostile only to threats)
- **Who:** A coven warden pair with two aligned Wildfolk.
- **Why here:** Rootweave-interior defense.
- **Wants:** Verification, escort-off, or elimination — in that order.
- **Drops:** (Combat only, very costly.) Rootweave-tapered staff, coven mantle, two sets of Wildfolk essence.

### Rootweave Drone (Construct-like, Earth/Aether, Shaping)
- **Who:** A Thornwood-cultivated wood-construct. Not sapient. Semi-autonomous pruning/defense.
- **Why here:** Maintains the living filament network.
- **Wants:** To prune. Perceives aggressive Unmaking as infection.
- **Drops:** Living wood component (high W, Shaping taper precursor), a seed-pod that will grow into a minor Rootweave fragment if planted at base.

## Drowned Coast / Tidegate Fringe (Rare tier — staged for Fairgean arc)

### Fairgean Raider (Humanoid, Water, hostile to surface by default pre-Trusted)
- **Who:** A Depth-Clan warrior — most likely from a clan ignoring Vorath's decree.
- **Why here:** Ancient grudge. Grief. Occasionally, actual raiding.
- **Wants:** Surface coin, surface captives for trade, surface bodies.
- **Drops:** Tidal-iron blade (W6–W8), Fairgean silk-cord, a shell-token (if recovered intact, turns into a Fairgean reputation hook).

### Shore-Revenant (Undead, Water/Aether)
- **Who:** Drowned sailor, risen. Not Fairgean. Not human anymore.
- **Why here:** The Drowned Ardweld ruins occasionally "wake" one.
- **Wants:** To return to a ship that no longer exists.
- **Drops:** Barnacled coin (junk), salt-bone (Water reagent), occasionally a ship's log fragment (lore item).

### Tide-Crab Swarm (Beast, Water, swarm)
- **Who:** A crust of palm-sized predators, opportunistic.
- **Why here:** Drowned bodies, always.
- **Wants:** Something dead. Something dying will do.
- **Drops:** Crab-shell (armor component, W2–W4), tidal reagent.
