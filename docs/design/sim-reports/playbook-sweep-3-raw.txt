PLAYBOOK: crafting-success
==========================================
=== playbook-runner: crafting-success — Crafting outcome distribution ===
Exposes the REAL outcome distribution of CraftingService.AttemptCraftAsync across skill/difficulty cells. Models a player who submits the UI-displayed (rounded-to-5) quantity — the common case when a user reads the recipe panel at face value.
