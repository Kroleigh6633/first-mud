# Faction Threads — Living Timelines (1,247 AS)

For each faction: **3 current storylines** (active, short-arc, 5–15 hours of content each) and **2 long-arc ambitions** (campaign-scale). Ending with a **Collision Map** showing where threads and NPCs overlap.

Flag: NPCs not named in `lore/factions.md` are additions. Storylines are current as of 1,247 AS and should be treated as the world state the player walks into.

---

## House Caervorn

### Current Storylines

1. **The Magic Census.** Aldric's Assayers are quietly building a registry of every person suspected of magical capacity in Caervorn territory, indexed by village. Ewan Truce of Marchkeep is one of five regional assessors. The registry is not yet being *acted* on — but the list has names on it, and someone in the Compact has been offered a copy for the right price.

2. **The Eastmile Garrison Proposal.** Aldric wants a permanent garrison in Eastmile — ostensibly bandit deterrence, actually a Thornwood-border listening post. Captain Bryn Hollemar has been asked to draft the logistics plan. He is deliberately slowing it.

3. **The Young Lord's Duel.** Aldric's cousin, Theyn Caervorn (eighteen, hot-tempered, a Shaping mage in secret), has publicly challenged a Compact factor who called Aldric a "highland butcher." The duel is scheduled. If Theyn wins, the Compact loses face. If Theyn loses, Theyn's secret magic will surface in the autopsy. Aldric does not know about the magic yet. Theyn's tutor does.

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **The Unified Crown.** Aldric intends to press for continental unification under Caervorn rule within five in-game years, using the banning of the Working as a justifying doctrine. Specific preparation: arming, census, and a coming assassination attempt on Eldest Mira Ashvale.
- **The Caervorn Secret Kept.** The Caervorn bloodline is *itself* magical (see `lore/factions.md`). Aldric knows. Hiding this while attacking magic elsewhere is the defining private terror of his reign. The long arc ends either in exposure (catastrophic) or in a deliberate, ritual self-stripping of the line's power — a project he has already consulted an Ashen Court agent about. He does not yet know who he consulted.

## The Thornwood Covens

### Current Storylines

1. **Rhianne Moss and the Unsanctioned Ward.** (Directly intersects Quest Chain 2.) An apprentice is attempting a village-warding that exceeds her authorization. Eldest Mira needs it stopped or sanctioned before the Rootweave strains.

2. **The Rootweave Retraction.** The Rootweave — the living magical network under the Thornwood — has been *withdrawing* from its outer edges for six months. Wardens report silences where song should be. Eldest Mira suspects sympathetic damage from something in the Gravenmarsh. She has asked the Gravenguard unofficially and been stonewalled.

3. **The Missing Apprentice.** Not Rhianne. A different apprentice, Gwyn Calder, vanished from the Thornwood Verge two months ago. The Covens assume Caervorn arrest. The truth is that Gwyn walked willingly into the Ashen Reach after receiving a Dream-Walker contact. She believed she was being called. Eldest Mira does not know yet.

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **The Eighth Century.** The Thornwood Covens have been waiting eight centuries for *something* — canon is vague, and so is Mira. The long arc reveals that they are waiting for the return of a specific Ardweld figure whose consciousness was preserved in the Ardweld Remnant at the Sundering. The Covens' survival and their pacifism both trace to this. The player's brooch may be related.
- **Rootweave Expansion.** The Covens have been slowly, illegally, extending Rootweave filaments beyond the Thornwood's canonical edge — into Gravenmarsh waterways, under the Compact roads. If Aldric discovers this, it becomes casus belli. If the Gravenguard discovers it, it becomes an artifact-containment problem.

## The Emerald Compact

### Current Storylines

1. **The Dead Factor in Veldann.** A senior Compact Factor, Ilven Moreth, was found dead of "fever" in Veldann. Three other Factors have died of "fever" in the last year. The Council of Factors is not investigating — publicly. Privately, two Councillors have hired the player's Rider Post to run messages they will not trust to their own couriers.

2. **The Halven Orys File.** (Intersects Quest Chain 2, Beat 4.) Halven Orys, monthly peddler in Eastmile, has been selling witch-locations to Caervorn. The Compact Council's auditors have noticed his ledger irregularities and are debating whether to discipline him or use him.

3. **The Sending-Stone Question.** A junior factor in Portmere has asked — too loudly — why certain shipment manifests are routed through an unlisted warehouse that seems to use magical communication. Her name is Jena Pell. She is going to disappear within two months unless someone intervenes. This is the thread into the Compact's canon secret (Ardweld sending-stone infrastructure).

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **The Open Roads Doctrine.** The Compact's long ambition is to guarantee open trade between all factions, indefinitely, by becoming too deeply embedded in everyone's economy to be attacked. This means funding all sides of the brewing Caervorn/Thornwood conflict — which, at a certain point, will be noticed and resented by both.
- **The Resurrection of the Sending-Stone Network.** The Compact possesses functional Ardweld sending-stones. Their long arc is to quietly map the full Ardweld communication lattice and become the monopoly carrier of instantaneous message traffic across Aeldran. When (not if) this succeeds, the Compact ceases being a merchant confederation and becomes a continental utility.

## The Gravenguard

### Current Storylines

1. **The Lost Expedition.** (Intersects Quest Chain 1.) Three Honored members went into an Ardweld Remnant pocket and did not return. Commander Drest has suppressed the full report. Lieutenant Varn is trying to surface it — not from virtue, from ambition.

2. **The Asker's Stone Anomaly.** The Ardweld monolith on the Gravenmarsh Causeway has begun asking the same question of every visitor: *"Where is the one who names?"* Scouts are rotating off the post because the repetition is disturbing. Maerwyn the hedge-witch might know what it means if asked.

3. **The Smuggler Ring.** Someone is moving unrefined dravenite off the books, through the Gravenmarsh bogs, into Ashen Court hands. Scout Orla was on this case when the Lost Expedition swallowed her. Her brother Kesh is continuing the work informally.

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **Containment of the Remnant.** Gravenguard doctrine holds that every Ardweld Remnant pocket should be sealed rather than explored once catalogued. Drest has privately deviated — the Lost Expedition was the first "extraction" attempt. The long arc either validates or destroys the doctrine, and likely the order with it.
- **The Mediator Role.** The Gravenguard's ambition (Drest's, specifically) is to become the arbiter of the Golvari/surface reconciliation and, eventually, of a broader faction concord. This is political, not scholarly, and the rest of the order would be furious if they knew. See canon: *The Ambassador's Answer* reconciliation chain.

## The Fairgean

### Current Storylines

1. **The Inland Walkers.** Fairgean scouts have been seen in the Gravenmarsh, in *human* clothing (badly worn). They are not raiding. They are looking for someone. The Compact has gotten a look at one and lived.

2. **The Drowned Envoy.** A Fairgean envoy named Aesk-of-the-Sunken-Stair attempted to reach Gravenhold two months ago. He was intercepted by what he believes was a Caervorn patrol; survived; is hiding in the Tidegate ruins; wants a surface intermediary to try again.

3. **Tide-Speaker Vorath's Decree.** Vorath has issued a rare public decree to the Depth-Clans: *no surface killing without Tide-Speaker sanction*. This is the Hostile → Unknown shift the player's Fairgean work will depend on. Some Clans are ignoring the decree. Their names are circulating.

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **The Abyss Retreat.** The Fairgean's inland walk is not exploration — it is **evacuation reconnaissance**. Something in the deep trenches is displacing entire Depth-Clan territories. The long arc is the Fairgean requesting, and receiving or being refused, surface resettlement rights. No precedent exists.
- **The Drowned Ardweld Restoration.** Beneath Vorath's reserve is a two-hundred-year project: the Fairgean have been quietly rebuilding Drowned Ardweld infrastructure, intending one day to re-light the Tidegate ward-network. This would make the Fairgean a magical power equal to the Thornwood — and they know nobody on the surface would welcome that.

## The Golvari

### Current Storylines

1. **The Ambassador's Silence.** Deep-Speaker Keth sent an ambassador to Gravenhold six months ago. The ambassador, *Kheth-vel-Muir*, is still there, in formal quarters, receiving no audience. He is running out of patience and out of his own supplies. He has begun taking walks.

2. **The Warren Collapse.** A section of the upper Golvari warrens collapsed under unusual circumstances — not natural subsidence. Survivors report hearing a *voice* in the rock before it gave. Stone-Speakers are investigating. They suspect Abyss-Thing pressure from below.

3. **The Fire-Iron Embargo.** The Stone-Speakers have secretly embargoed Golvari Fireiron export to the surface. Any Fireiron now reaching the Compact market is being smuggled by specific Golvari clans, against caste authority. The price has quadrupled. Compact smiths are furious. Caervorn is privately pleased (less magical weaponry circulating).

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **Surface Habitation Rights.** The Golvari long arc is a formal treaty for upper-warren zones to be recognized as Golvari sovereign territory, including some surface emergence points. This is what the ambassador was sent to negotiate. It will not succeed without the player's intervention on the Reconciliation chain.
- **The True Deep Defense.** Above and below it all, the Golvari are fortifying against whatever the Abyss-Things are. They cannot win that fight alone. The long arc forces them into alliance — with the Fairgean (ancient enemies), with the Gravenguard (professional rivals), or with no one (and they lose).

## The Ashen Court

### Current Storylines

1. **Maren's Silence.** Lord-Assessor Maren has been unseen for nine months. Ashen Court activity has *not* stopped — if anything, it has gotten more targeted. Three Compact Factors dead. One Thornwood apprentice (Gwyn Calder) recruited via Dream. One Caervorn assessor (anonymous, in the Assay Office) turned. Something is being staged.

2. **The Dream Installations.** Ashen agents are installing Dream-Walking relay nodes across Aeldran — they look like ordinary shrines or milestones. At least one is in Eastmile. The chapel defacement in the evolved zone-state is the marker.

3. **The Seed-Conversion Program.** The Ashen Court is running a small, careful program of converting *children* of minor nobility to Dream-contact — not Soul-Touching, not yet. Cultivation. The program is visible if a player spends enough time in the Ashen Reach; canon is silent on children specifically, so **flag this as an addition** for user approval.

### Long-Arc Ambitions

- **The Completion of the Sundering.** Per canon, the Ashen Court believes the Sundering was transformation and should be *completed*. Long arc: the coordinated Dream-Walk of every Aeldran leader in a single night, simultaneous Soul-Touching at scale. The infrastructure required is what the Dream-installations are building toward.
- **The Inheritor Claim.** The Ashen Court's public, long-arc claim is that they are the legitimate Ardweld successor state. Maren intends, at the endpoint, to formally petition the Ardweld Remnant's Preservers for recognition. This is the strategic reason the Lost Expedition had to be eliminated: Drest's people were about to make that petition first.

## Collision Map

**Threads that touch each other:**

- *Caervorn Magic Census* ↔ *Compact's "File on Orys"* ↔ *Thornwood's Missing Apprentice*. The registry, the informant, and the vanished. One corrupt factor is the hinge.
- *Gravenguard Lost Expedition* ↔ *Ashen Court Inheritor Claim*. The Expedition was killed because it was about to petition the Preservers first.
- *Fairgean Abyss Retreat* ↔ *Golvari True Deep Defense*. Same threat, opposite sides of the world, ancient enemies.
- *Thornwood Rootweave Retraction* ↔ *Gravenguard Asker's Stone Anomaly*. Sympathetic Ardweld-era damage linking Thornwood and Gravenmarsh geography.
- *Caervorn Young Lord's Duel* ↔ *Compact's Dead Factor*. Theyn's duel is with a factor whose partner was the last factor who died of "fever." Compact assassinations, Caervorn politics, one stage.
- *Caervorn Secret Kept* ↔ *Ashen Court Completion of the Sundering*. Aldric's private consultation about stripping his bloodline's magic is being run by an Ashen agent who is using it as a vector into Caervorn command.

- **Halven Orys** — Compact factor, Thornwood informant seller, Ashen Court peripheral contact.
- **Ewan Truce** — Caervorn assessor, Gravenguard tangential (owns an illegal artifact), Ashen Court's *actual* turned asset (one of the five assessors — him).
- **Brother Velm** — Eastmile chapel, Thornwood ex-acolyte, Gravenguard consultant (Chain 1), Ashen Court target (Dream-Walked if player delays).

- **Theyn Caervorn** — Caervorn noble, closet Shaping mage (Thornwood latent recruitment target), currently facing a Compact duel.
