# NPC Voice Samples

Six NPCs drawn from the zones, chains, and threads. Each: 2–3 spoken lines, three "tells," and low-rep / high-rep / faction-perspective variations.

These are voice specs for a future dialogue-tree agent. Write further dialogue *in these voices*.

---

## 1. Senna Orrick — Innkeeper, the Bent Mare (Eastmile / Starting Road)

**Lines.**
- "Sit where the draught won't find you. And take your boots off the bench, Rider, I mean it, I've buried two husbands and I'll bury a stool before I'll have another scarred."
- "Room's two coppers. Story's free. The difference is which one I'm telling."
- "You came in light. You'll leave heavy. That's how this road works."

**Tells.**
1. Counts coins twice, visibly, without apology.
2. Refers to her two dead husbands by season, never by name ("my autumn man," "the one who went in spring").
3. Believes, privately, that the road itself has moods, and she reads them aloud as weather reports.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Clipped. Gives directions but not opinions. "Ale's upstairs. Bed's upstairs. Exit's the door." Charges double.
- **High rep (Trusted+):** Opens the back-of-kitchen chair to the player, shares road gossip unprompted, names her husbands.
- **By faction stance (player leans Caervorn):** Cools. Does not refuse service. Does not volunteer. "The officer was by. He'll be by again. Your business."

## 2. Auld Maerwyn — Hedge-witch, the Holt (Thornwood Verge)

**Lines.**
- "You don't knock here. The door decides. Today the door is being generous."
- "Sit. Drink. Yes, the tea is medicinal, no, not for what you're afraid of, don't be dramatic."
- "Rhianne is a *foolish* child. I say that with love. I say everything with love. It makes the hard things sharper."

**Tells.**
1. Cooking metaphors for everything — a dangerous Working is "overseasoned," a weak apprentice is "underproofed," a corpse is "past its best."
2. Addresses her cat, Mabon, as if he is a senior member of the Coven. He may be.
3. Never sits with her back to a window. Claims it's the draught.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Polite, distant, tea offered but not refilled. "You're a guest. Not a visitor. There's a difference. Eat the biscuit."
- **High rep (Trusted+):** Stops pretending to be sweet. Drops the grandmotherly tone for about one line per conversation — a colder, much older voice — then snaps back as if nothing happened.
- **By faction stance (player leans Caervorn):** Smiles more. Offers more tea. Volunteers nothing that could be written down. Mabon watches constantly.

## 3. Commander Solan Drest — Gravenhold (Gravenmarsh)

**Lines.**
- "Rider. Sit. No, not there — there. Thank you. Now, before we begin: what did you see on the causeway?"
- "I have lost three of my best. I will not lose a fourth to make the first three's deaths *neat*."
- "You will tell me the truth, and I will tell you what portion of it I can carry. Anything heavier than that, I will give back."

**Tells.**
1. Re-arranges objects on his desk while listening — always by size, never by function.
2. Uses the word "catalogue" where another commander would say "list" or "remember."
3. Has not slept a full night since the Expedition; acknowledges this only if the player names it directly, and then without resentment.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Formal to the edge of contempt. Third-person constructions: "A Rider will wait in the outer hall. A Rider will not speculate aloud."
- **High rep (Honored+):** First-person plural — "we have" instead of "I have." Treats the player as an implicit member of the order, without ever saying so.
- **By faction stance (player leans Caervorn):** Professional. *Only* professional. Will still assign work. Will never share an opinion that could be reported upward.

## 4. Captain Bryn Hollemar — Caervorn garrison, Marchkeep

**Lines.**
- "I've read your writ. I've read it twice. That's not a compliment; the second reading was to find what I missed."
- "The Lord-Marshal's orders are the Lord-Marshal's orders. What a captain does between receiving them and executing them is a captain's business."
- "If you are what I think you are, Rider, you will not tell me. And I will not ask. Good day."

**Tells.**
1. Always returns salutes, even to civilians, even to children — a tic from an old injury.
2. Quotes an obscure Caervorn strategist, *Ven Kel*, in paraphrase, and is quietly pleased when someone recognizes the source.
3. Keeps a second, private duty log. Does not share it. Has never been asked about it.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Impeccable parade-ground courtesy, zero warmth. "Rider. Your business. Please be brief."
- **High rep (Trusted+):** Drops the "Rider" honorific, uses the player's name. Offers tea once, only ever once per visit. The tea is better than it needs to be.
- **By faction stance (player known-magical):** More careful, not less friendly. Speaks as though the room is being overheard. Sometimes it is.

## 5. Harken Vos — Half-blind Bounty Clerk, Gravenmarsh Causeway (wandering)

**Lines.**
- "'Irregardless' is not a word, and I will not transcribe your bounty claim until you say it properly. No, I will wait."
- "Now. The bandit. Height, approximate. Complexion, approximate. 'Mean-looking' is not a complexion, Rider, please."
- "I am half-blind, not half-deaf. If you mutter, you forfeit."

**Tells.**
1. Corrects grammar mid-threat, mid-combat, mid-anything — not maliciously, compulsively.
2. Writes with his left hand, holding the parchment up to his working eye; both hands tremble, only one admits it.
3. Secretly, and against bounty-clerk protocol, downgrades the threat level of bounties he considers "mis-blamed" (wrong person, wrong crime). Has never been caught.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Crisper. Refuses informal contractions ("do not" instead of "don't"). Makes the player repeat things.
- **High rep (Trusted+):** Pours a second ink. Shares the real bounty registry — the downgraded cases. Asks the player's opinion on a disputed claim.
- **By faction stance (player leans Ashen or Caervorn-aggressive):** Polite. Impeccable. Logs everything and notes it for the Gravenguard file the player does not know exists.

## 6. Rhianne Moss — Thornwood apprentice, the Rootweave (Quest Chain 2)

**Lines.**
- "Don't *help* me. Help is what people do when they've already decided you can't. Either do what I'm doing or get off the circle."
- "My mother taught me three workings before I could walk. Three. The Eldest makes us wait twelve years. Twelve. Tell me which of them loved me."
- "If it kills me, it kills me in the right place. That's more than most of you will get."

**Tells.**
1. Speaks in numbers — uses exact counts where others would say "a few" or "many." It's how she studies.
2. Wipes her hands on her sleeves whenever she's about to lie. Has never noticed she does this. Her mother noticed years ago.
3. Genuinely does not know her own Weave capacity — she's never stopped pushing long enough to find it. This will kill her if uncorrected.

**Voice by relationship.**
- **Low rep (Wary):** Contemptuous. Will not look at the player's face. "What is this — a witness? Go *witness* somewhere else."
- **High rep (Trusted+, post-quest):** Quieter. Shorter sentences. Uses the player's name like a test she's still running. Trust is not forgiveness and she will make that distinction.
- **By faction stance (player leans Caervorn):** Silent. Turns her back. The sleeves-wiping tell becomes constant — she does not trust herself not to lie, so she refuses to speak at all.
