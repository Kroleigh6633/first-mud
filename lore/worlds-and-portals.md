# Worlds and Portal System

## Overview

Aeldran is the first world. Others exist — pocket dimensions, alternate planes, deep civilizations, and the spiritual substrate beneath reality. Each is a complete ecosystem with its own factions, crafting materials, quest chains, hireable NPCs, loot tables, and magic expression rules.

Portals are not discovered. They are **earned**.

---

## Portal Unlock Process (All Worlds)

Earning access to a new world is a three-stage quest chain:

| Stage | Name | What It Requires | Result |
|-------|------|-----------------|--------|
| **1 — Discovery** | Learn the portal exists | Specific reputation threshold + story beat | Portal location revealed on map |
| **2 — Stabilization** | Make the portal traversable | Materials (often world-specific) + a Working | One-way travel becomes possible |
| **3 — Anchoring** | Create your personal teleport | A deeper quest chain + anchor materials | Permanent fast travel shortcut unlocked |

Until Stage 3 is complete, reaching the portal requires traveling the road every time. The road is dangerous. The portal is worth protecting.

Later worlds require reputation in *multiple* worlds — not just Aeldran. The game expands outward, never inward.

## The Worlds

---

### World 1: Aeldran
*The Starting World*

The continent described in full in `world-aeldran.md`. All players begin here. All portals branch from here initially.

**Magic rules**: Standard — all elements, both polarities, Weave functions normally.

---

### World 2: The Ardweld Remnant
*Pocket Dimensions Left from the Sundering*

**Nature**: Not a single place — a collection of fractured dimensional pockets, each preserving a fragment of the Ardweld civilization at the moment of the Sundering. Some are frozen in time. Some are actively collapsing. Some contain things that survived.

**Discovery unlock**: Gravenguard Honored + recovery of an Ardweld portal key from a specific ruin.
**Stabilization requires**: Dravenite refined chips (uncommon), Aether Shaping Tier 3 Working.
**Anchoring requires**: A specific Gravenguard Bound questline — *What the Lost Expedition Found*.

**Magic rules**: Modified — Unmaking is dominant here. Shaping Workings cost more Weave. Unmaking Workings cost less but have unpredictable secondary effects. Twice-Born mages are unusually powerful here. Pure Shaping mages face meaningful challenge.

**Unique features**:
- Ardweld automata still active — some hostile, some neutral if approached correctly
- The Ardweld's own records, preserved — answer questions about the Sundering's true cause
- Dravenite in its refined state — most potent focus material in the game, dangerous to carry
- Ward-engine schematics (unlocks new automation tier unavailable in Aeldran)
- Each pocket dimension is procedurally generated with thematic consistency — infinite exploration

**Factions (Remnant-specific)**:
- **The Preservers**: Ardweld construct intelligences maintaining the pockets. Neutral by default. Honored access opens their archives.
- **The Unraveled**: Humanoids who stumbled into the Remnant centuries ago and adapted to the Unmaking-dominant environment. Hostile initially. Strange.

**Unique crafting materials**:
- Sundering Shards (weapon components — unstable, powerful)
- Preserved Ardweld Alloy (armor — lightweight, magical resistance)
- Resonance Cores (automation components — vastly superior)

---

### World 3: The Fairgean Deep
*Undersea Civilization*

**Nature**: The Fairgean's full civilization, accessible only underwater. Vast, ancient, architecturally stunning in ways no surface dweller has seen in two centuries. Also currently under threat from something in the deepest trenches.

**Discovery unlock**: Fairgean Trusted (itself a significant achievement — requires the Hostile → Unknown quest chain first).
**Stabilization requires**: Fairgean-specific tidal materials + Water Working Tier 2.
**Anchoring requires**: Fairgean Honored + a quest to resolve what drove them inland.

**Magic rules**: Water-primary mages are significantly amplified. Fire-primary mages are suppressed (not disabled — just harder). Weave regeneration is faster near deep thermal vents. Air Working barely functions.

**Unique features**:
- Pressure mechanics — certain areas require crafted pressure gear to survive
- The Drowned Ardweld ruins — an Ardweld coastal city, perfectly preserved underwater, undisturbed
- Bioluminescent aesthetic — ASCII rendered in blue/green palette
- Tidal rhythm — in-world time cycles affect what areas are accessible

**Factions (Deep-specific)**:
- **The Fairgean Depth-Clans**: Multiple clans with their own internal politics. Surface reputation carries over but each clan is separate.
- **The Abyss-Things**: What is in the deep trenches. Not a faction. A threat. Understanding them is a multi-world questline.

**Unique crafting materials**:
- Tidal Iron (harder than surface iron, water-Shaping enhanced)
- Deep Coral (reagent — Water Working amplifier)
- Abyss Residue (dangerous — Unmaking-aligned, dropped by Abyss-Things)
- Fairgean Silkweed (cloth — lightweight armor, exceptional flexibility)

---

### World 4: The Golvari Deeps
*Underground Golvari Civilization*

**Nature**: The Golvari's full world — vast underground cave systems, fungal forests, underground seas, cities carved into living rock. A complete civilization that has never needed the surface. Currently crowded from below by whatever the Abyss-Things are pushing up.

**Discovery unlock**: Golvari Honored + the Golvari / Gravenguard reconciliation quest.
**Stabilization requires**: Earth Working Tier 2 + Golvari stone-craft materials.
**Anchoring requires**: Golvari Bound questline — *Into the True Deep*.

**Magic rules**: Earth and Fire Shaping amplified. Air Working non-functional (no sky). Water Working behaves differently — underground water has different magical properties.

**Unique features**:
- Mining depth system — deeper layers have better materials but worse danger
- Fungal ecosystem — unique crafting reagents available nowhere else
- Golvari crafting masters as hireable NPCs (best smiths in any world)
- The True Deep entrance — what is down there connects to the Fairgean's Abyss-Things

**Factions (Deeps-specific)**:
- **The Stone-Speakers**: Golvari mage caste — Earth/Fire Working specialists. Knowledge exchange is possible.
- **The Deeprunners**: Golvari explorers who go into the True Deep and come back changed. Some of them.

**Unique crafting materials**:
- Deepstone (structural material — best armor base material in the game)
- Golvari Fireiron (weapon material — Earth/Fire hybrid, unusual properties)
- Fungal Weave (alchemical reagent — broad spectrum uses)
- Deep Crystal (rare focus stone — Earth-primary alternative to Dravenite, no warp risk, less powerful)

---

### World 5: The Wyrd-Paths
*Spirit Plane — Home of the Wildfolk*

**Nature**: The spiritual substrate beneath Aeldran — a version of the same geography, but as the Wildfolk experience it. Emotional, symbolic, dangerous to those without Wyrd-awareness. Time behaves differently. Distance is measured in meaning, not meters.

**Discovery unlock**: Aether Working Tier 3 + a specific Wyrd quest chain (available when tangle accumulates or when the player actively pursues their wyrd).
**Stabilization requires**: Aether Shaping Working + Wildfolk companion bond depth (Companion Layer 4+).
**Anchoring requires**: Wyrd-Paths Honored + resolution of a personal wyrd thread.

**Magic rules**: Aether Working is the only Working that functions reliably. Other elements manifest as emotional states rather than practical effects. Weave regenerates instantly here but overuse causes wyrd-tangle directly.

**Unique features**:
- Wyrd resolution quests — past choices made visible and confrontable
- Wildfolk social structures — the politics of elemental spirits
- Lost companions (Wildfolk who left due to betrayal) can be found and reconciled here
- Tangle clearing — the only reliable way to reduce serious tangle accumulation
- Personal history is visible as landscape — the player's wyrd is the terrain

**Factions (Wyrd-Paths-specific)**:
- **The Anchored**: Wildfolk who have bonded deeply with mages across centuries. Ancient. Wise. Strange.
- **The Untethered**: Wildfolk that have never bonded. Wild, unpredictable, not hostile but not safe.
- **The Tangled**: Wyrd-distorted entities that were once people. Cautionary tales made manifest.

**Unique crafting materials**:
- Wyrd-Thread (woven from resolved fate — quest material, not lootable)
- Wildfolk Essence (companion-craft only — deepens companion layers, can't be traded)
- Aether Silk (the physical form of emotional resonance — rare, strange, powerful)
- Memory-Stone (records a moment permanently — unique item creation material)

---

### World 6: The Dream
*Ashen Court's Power Source — Late Game*

**Nature**: The realm that Dream-Walkers access. The Ashen Court has been operating here for centuries, building infrastructure in a place where most people cannot go. It is the source of Lord-Assessor Maren's power and the staging ground for the Ashen Court's long plan.

**Discovery unlock**: Automatic — the player is Dream-Walked by an Ashen Court agent before they have any ability to resist. The world is known from the inside out, as a victim.
**Stabilization requires**: Aether Working Tier 4 + either a Gravenguard Bound quest or a morally-compromising Ashen Court quest.
**Anchoring requires**: Defeating the Ashen Court's Dream infrastructure — a multi-stage late-game questline.

**Magic rules**: Dream-Walking is the primary Working. Unmaking is fully accessible to any mage here regardless of normal polarity. All Working costs double Weave. Nothing you do here is permanent unless you anchor it with specific materials.

**Unique features**:
- The Ashen Court's hidden base of operations
- Lord-Assessor Maren's stronghold
- The final pieces of the Sundering's true cause
- Dream-Walking becomes an offensive tool once anchored
- Ashen Court's Soul-Touched servants — some can be freed

**Factions (Dream-specific)**:
- **The Freed**: Soul-Touched individuals whose will has been partially restored. Grateful. Broken in specific ways. Helpful.
- **The Court of Shadows**: The Ashen Court's Dream-presence — more powerful here than anywhere else.

**Unique crafting materials**:
- Dream-Iron (unstable outside the Dream unless properly anchored)
- Nightmare Residue (Aether/Unmaking reagent — very powerful, ethically ambiguous)
- The Waking Stone (allows Dream materials to persist in Aeldran — rare, quest-reward only)

## World Progression Summary

```
Aeldran (start)
    ↓ Gravenguard Honored
Ardweld Remnant (World 2)
    ↓ Fairgean Trusted (separate chain)
Fairgean Deep (World 3)
    ↓ Golvari Honored + Reconciliation quest
Golvari Deeps (World 4)
    ↓ Aether Tier 3 + Wyrd chain
Wyrd-Paths (World 5)
    ↓ Ashen Court attacks (automatic trigger, late game)
The Dream (World 6)
```

Worlds 3 and 4 can be pursued in parallel with World 2. World 5 can begin whenever the player's magic depth supports it. World 6 is introduced by the antagonist — the player doesn't choose when they first enter.
