# Magic System: The Working

## Overview

Magic in Aeldran is called **The Working**. It is not mystical in the sense of being unknowable — it follows rules, responds to study, and can be learned. But it is not safe. The line between a mage and a cautionary tale is thinner than most practitioners admit.

---

## Fundamental Polarity

Every magical act is either **Shaping** or **Unmaking**. This is not a choice the mage makes — it is what they *are*.

| Polarity | Nature | Expression |
|----------|--------|------------|
| **Shaping** (Additive) | Creates, enhances, binds, heals, builds | The vast majority of mages. Considered safe. |
| **Unmaking** (Subtractive) | Destroys, dissolves, removes, unmakes | Rare. Feared. Persecuted in most kingdoms. |
| **Twice-Born** | Full Working — both polarities | Extremely rare. Possibly one per generation. World-altering. |

A Shaping mage cannot learn Unmaking through study. It either is or is not present. The same in reverse. A Twice-Born cannot suppress either polarity — both are always present, always active, and the tension between them is constant and exhausting.

The player discovers their polarity during the first significant magical encounter. It cannot be predicted in advance.

## The Five Elements

Every act of the Working aligns to one of five elements. A mage's **primary affinity** is revealed at first casting and cannot be changed. Secondary affinities can be developed through research and practice but are always weaker.

### Fire
- **Shaping**: Forge-craft, warmth, light, courage, battle-fire wards
- **Unmaking**: Consume utterly — no ash, no trace, no memory of the thing

### Water
- **Shaping**: Healing, flow, divination, memory preservation, emotional soothing
- **Unmaking**: Drown meaning, dissolve memory, unmake bonds between people

### Earth
- **Shaping**: Binding, growth, golem-craft, warding, structural reinforcement
- **Unmaking**: Collapse, rot, age rapidly, return to undifferentiated matter

### Air
- **Shaping**: Speed, sending (magical communication), illusion, weather influence
- **Unmaking**: Silence permanently, erase sound from a moment in time, steal breath

### Aether
- **Shaping**: Spirit-touch, wyrd-sight, soul communication, Wildfolk bonding, dream observation
- **Unmaking**: Soul-Touching (Confessor magic), Dream-Walking (offensive), will dissolution, permanent memory removal

Aether/Unmaking is the most dangerous and most forbidden. The Ashen Court's power base.

## The Weave

Every living being with magical capacity has a **Weave** — an internal reservoir of magical force, visible to trained mages as a faint shimmer around the body.

### Properties
- Depletes with use. Regenerates with rest, food, and proximity to dravenite.
- Overdrawn Weave causes: hair/eye color shift (early), tremors (moderate), accelerated aging (severe), madness (critical), dissolution (fatal)
- The Weave's visible state communicates to others: full Weave = shimmer; empty Weave = grey, dull, visibly diminished
- NPCs notice. A depleted Rider is a vulnerable Rider.

### Weave Capacity
Starts small. Grows through use — but only when pushed near the limit, not safely exercised. The mages who grow fastest are the ones who take risks. This has a cost.

### Focus Stones — Dravenite
Veins of a deep mineral called **dravenite** (from Ardweld: *draven* = to shape/work). Dravenite:
- Amplifies the Weave — temporarily extends capacity
- Reduces Weave regeneration cost
- Prolonged heavy use warps the caster: first cosmetically, then cognitively, then physically
- The Ardweld's refinement techniques could control the warp. Those techniques are lost.
- Raw dravenite is less dangerous. Refined dravenite chips (found in ruins) are potent and risky.

## Wildfolk

Elemental spirits that exist in the same world as the player, invisible to most, visible to Aether-sensitive mages. They predate the Ardweld.

### Nature
- Not intelligent in the human sense — more like very complicated weather systems with preference
- Each Wildfolk aligns to one element
- They attach to mages whose Weave resonates with them
- Cannot be summoned reliably — they choose
- Once attached, they are loyal to a fault and permanent (barring betrayal)

### Companion Role
Wildfolk are the first type of companion most players encounter. They are not combat powerhouses early. They are observers, warners, and resonators — their presence amplifies their aligned element in the player's Working.

A Fire Wildfolk attached to a Water-primary mage is possible — rare, strange, creates unusual hybrid effects.

### Betrayal
If a mage draws deeply on Aether/Unmaking in a Wildfolk's presence without cause, the Wildfolk leaves. They do not return unless the player undertakes a specific Wyrd-Paths world quest to find them.

## The Wyrd System

Every person in Aeldran has a **wyrd** — a fate-thread. Most people cannot see it. Aether mages can sense wyrd in others. Certain Ardweld artifacts display it visually.

### Mechanics
- Major story arc completions **settle wyrd** — reduce tangle
- Ignoring wyrd-quests (flagged when available) accumulates **tangle**
- Tangle manifests as: persistent bad luck, haunting visions (brief NPC interactions that reference past choices), cursed rolls in specific situations
- Heavy tangle: NPCs react strangely, wyrd-sensitive companions become uneasy, certain quests become unavailable
- Tangle can be resolved through Wyrd-Paths world quests — but it compounds the longer it is left

### Player Choice
Wyrd is not mandatory. A player can ignore it entirely and pursue empire-like crafting goals. The world does not force resolution. It simply notices, and over time, the noticing accumulates into a presence.

---

## Coven-Working (Group Magic)

When multiple mages combine their Weave into a single Working, power amplifies non-linearly. Two mages together can achieve what ten working separately cannot.

### Rules
- Requires trust — magical and personal. A coven partner who harbors ill intent destabilizes the Working.
- Betrayal during a coven-Working is catastrophic for both parties: Weave backlash, potential permanent capacity reduction.
- Coven-Working is not reliably available solo — it requires NPC companion mages at sufficient relationship depth.
- Thornwood Covens teach it formally. Learning it alone from texts risks the backlash without the support.

## Forbidden Arts

| Art | Element | Why Forbidden |
|-----|---------|---------------|
| **Soul-Touching** | Aether/Unmaking | Unmakes the target's will. Irreversible. Ashen Court weapon. |
| **Dream-Walking** | Aether/Shaping | Enters another's dreams uninvited. Creates terror, installs false memories. Ashen Court uses offensively. |
| **Full Unmaking** | Any/Unmaking (extreme) | Removes something from existence entirely — including its history. Theoretical. The Sundering may have involved this. |
| **Wyrd-Cutting** | Aether/Unmaking | Severs a person's wyrd, leaving them fate-blind and lucky in the short term, catastrophically unlucky over years. |

All four are illegal in every kingdom, including the Ashen Court (which uses them anyway, internally).

## Research Tree Structure

Magic research is personal advancement. Studying unlocks deeper schools, lost Ardweld techniques, and new Working expressions.

### Tier Structure
- **Tier 1**: Element basics, Weave management, focus stone use
- **Tier 2**: Secondary affinity development, Wildfolk communication, coven introduction
- **Tier 3**: Deep element expression, Wyrd-sight, advanced golem-craft
- **Tier 4**: Cross-element Working, Ardweld technique fragments
- **Tier 5**: Aether/Unmaking schools (both forbidden — require special quest chain to unlock legally/safely)
- **Tier 6**: Twice-Born techniques — only accessible if the player is Twice-Born

Research requires: time, materials (reagents, texts, dravenite), and often a mentor NPC (gated on faction reputation).
