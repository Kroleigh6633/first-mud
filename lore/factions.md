# Factions of Aeldran

## Reputation Tiers (All Factions)

| Tier | Name | Access |
|------|------|--------|
| -2 | **Hostile** | Attacked on sight in faction territory |
| -1 | **Wary** | No quests, merchants price-gouge, faction members avoid |
| 0 | **Unknown** | Default start. Basic interactions only. |
| 1 | **Known** | Fetch quests, basic merchant access, faction gossip |
| 2 | **Trusted** | Named quests, merchant discounts, faction safe houses |
| 3 | **Honored** | Complex quest chains, faction abilities/spells unlocked, inner circle |
| 4 | **Bound** | Wyrd-level quests, deepest lore, faction artifacts, mentor access |

Reputation is earned through: quest completion, material donations, combat support, and wyrd-aligned choices. It is lost through: working against faction interests, killing faction members, breaking faction-specific oaths.

## The Factions

---

### House Caervorn
*The Highland Kingdom*

**Base location**: The Ironspire, Caervorn Highlands
**Current leader**: Lord-Marshal Aldric Caervorn (young, ambitious, dangerous)
**Disposition to magic**: Actively hostile. Magic use in Caervorn territory is illegal.
**Tension**: Hostile ↔ Thornwood Covens, Wary ↔ Golvari

**Character**: Old noble blood, proud, militaristic. They believe the Sundering proved magic destroys civilization. They are not entirely wrong — but they are weaponizing that belief. Aldric wants to unify Aeldran under Caervorn rule and ban the Working continent-wide.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: Access to Caervorn roads (safer than alternatives)
- Trusted: Caervorn arms and armor (excellent non-magical gear)
- Honored: Information on Ardweld ruins that Caervorn has *suppressed*
- Bound: The truth about why Caervorn nobility is actually afraid of magic (they have it, and they know it)

**Tension Cost**: Each tier of Caervorn rep costs Thornwood Coven rep. A Caervorn Honored player is a Thornwood Wary player.

**Unique mechanic**: Gaining Caervorn rep as a mage requires actively hiding or downplaying magical ability. Some quests require not using magic. Tension between who you are and who they need you to be.

---

### The Thornwood Covens
*Matriarchal Mage Collective*

**Base location**: The Rootweave, deep Thornwood forest
**Current leader**: Eldest Mira Ashvale (ancient, patient, has seen worse than Aldric)
**Disposition to magic**: Welcoming. Reverent of the Weave.
**Tension**: Hostile ↔ House Caervorn, Suspicious ↔ Ashen Court

**Character**: Celtic/matriarchal. Magic is not a weapon or a tool — it is a responsibility. The Covens preserve knowledge, train mages, and maintain the Rootweave (a living magical network embedded in the Thornwood that amplifies Working within the forest). They have secrets they've kept for eight centuries.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: Healing services, basic reagent access
- Trusted: Coven-Working training access, Rootweave navigation
- Honored: Tier 3 magic research unlocked, Wildfolk communication techniques
- Bound: The Covens' true purpose — they are not just preserving knowledge. They are waiting for something.

**Unique mechanic**: Coven rep grows faster when you use Shaping magic openly and help mages in distress. Unmaking magic use in the Thornwood is noticed and commented on — not banned, but watched.

---

### The Emerald Compact
*Merchant Confederation*

**Base location**: Three free cities — Portmere, Veldann, and Ashcross — along the southern coast
**Current leader**: Council of Factors (rotating, contentious, compromise-driven)
**Disposition to magic**: Neutral/pragmatic. Magic is useful, fine, whatever — can we talk about the tariff?
**Tension**: Competitive ↔ Fairgean (trade routes), Neutral ↔ everyone else

**Character**: The Compact wants the roads open and the ledgers balanced. They fund every faction's intelligence network simultaneously and make no apologies for it. They are not evil — they are practical in ways that occasionally produce evil outcomes. The best gear is available here. So is information, for a price.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: Access to Compact markets (extensive)
- Trusted: Discounted trade goods, access to Compact intelligence (buy information)
- Honored: Trade golem purchase/upgrade access, Compact safe houses continent-wide
- Bound: The Compact's actual power source — they have Ardweld sending-stone infrastructure that still functions, and they know it

**Unique mechanic**: Compact rep grows through economic activity — trading, crafting for sale, quest completion for coin. Losing Compact rep through poor trading behavior or unpaid debts is possible.

---

### The Gravenguard
*Ardweld Artifact Hunters / Military-Scholarly Order*

**Base location**: Gravenhold, the Gravenmarsh
**Current leader**: Commander Solan Drest (cautious, recently shaken, hiding something)
**Disposition to magic**: Professionally neutral. Studies it. Doesn't fully trust it.
**Tension**: Suspicious ↔ Golvari, Wary ↔ Ashen Court

**Character**: Part explorer, part archivist, part soldier. The Gravenguard's mission is to find, document, and *contain* Ardweld artifacts before they cause harm or fall into the wrong hands. They are the closest thing Aeldran has to a neutral faction. They lost an expedition recently. Commander Drest is not sleeping.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: Access to Gravenguard artifact records (partial)
- Trusted: Maps to Ardweld ruin sites, Ardweld artifact identification service
- Honored: Portal quest chain introduction (the Gravenguard knows portals exist)
- Bound: What the lost expedition found — and why Drest hasn't told anyone

**Unique mechanic**: Gravenguard rep requires demonstrating competence, not just task completion. Failing a Gravenguard quest is worse than not attempting it.

---

### The Fairgean
*Sea-Folk, Amphibious, Ancient Grudge*

**Base location**: The Drowned Coast, undersea settlements
**Current leader**: Tide-Speaker Vorath (speaks rarely, means everything they say)
**Disposition to magic**: Different relationship with the Working entirely — theirs functions partially underwater
**Tension**: Hostile ↔ surface dwellers (default), Competitive ↔ Compact, Deep Wary ↔ Golvari

**Character**: The Fairgean are not monsters. They are a civilization with a two-hundred-year grievance that has never been properly addressed. They have been seen inland recently for the first time in centuries. Something has driven them from the deep. They are not going to explain themselves to anyone who isn't Trusted.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: They stop trying to kill you on sight (achievement in itself)
- Trusted: Trade access — Fairgean materials unavailable anywhere else
- Honored: Fairgean Deep portal quest chain begins
- Bound: What drove them inland. Why the deep changed. What they found in the Drowned Ardweld ruins.

**Unique mechanic**: Getting from Hostile → Unknown requires a specific quest chain and cannot be done through normal means. Most players encounter the Fairgean as enemies first.

---

### The Golvari
*Goblinoid Nation — Complex, Not Just Monsters*

**Base location**: The Maw and the Deeps below the Marches
**Current leader**: Deep-Speaker Keth (sent the ambassador six months ago — nobody knows what was said)
**Disposition to magic**: Earth and Fire Shaping aligned, naturally. Different Working expression than surface mages.
**Tension**: Hostile ↔ Gravenguard (territorial), Suspicious ↔ surface world, Deep Wary ↔ Fairgean

**Character**: Surface dwellers have treated the Golvari as monsters for centuries. The Golvari have occasionally behaved like monsters in response. The truth is a civilization under pressure from below (something in the deep Deeps) and pressure from above (surface expansion). The ambassador to Gravenhold was a peace offer — possibly the last one.

**Reputation Rewards**:
- Known: Safe passage through the upper Marches
- Trusted: Golvari crafting access — stone-work and fire-craft unavailable elsewhere
- Honored: Golvari Deeps portal quest chain, access to deep-resource loot tables
- Bound: What is in the deep Deeps driving the Golvari up. It is not good.

**Unique mechanic**: Golvari rep and Gravenguard rep are in direct tension — each tier of one costs a tier of the other until a specific Honored quest resolves the conflict between them.

---

### The Ashen Court
*Aether-Corrupted Former Nobility — The Antagonist*

**Base location**: The Pale City, the Ashen Reach
**Current leader**: Lord-Assessor Maren (Soul-Touched, Dream-Walker, has not been seen in nine months)
**Disposition to magic**: Worships it. Specifically Aether/Unmaking.
**Tension**: At war ↔ everyone (eventually)

**Character**: The Ashen Court are what the Ardweld could have become if they had survived with only their worst instincts intact. Former nobility who discovered Aether/Unmaking centuries ago and decided the correct response was power, domination, and Soul-Touching everyone who disagreed. They are not mindlessly evil — they have an ideology: the Unmaking is the truth of the world, Shaping is self-deception, and their purpose is to finish what the Sundering started.

Lord-Assessor Maren's nine-month silence is not retirement.

**Reputation Mechanics**: The Ashen Court cannot be befriended in the normal sense. There is a route to working with them — a specific deep questline that is genuinely morally compromising. It unlocks Tier 5 magic research faster than any other path. The cost is visible to every other faction.

**Unique mechanic**: As the main antagonist faction, the Ashen Court's activity level increases with player progression. They notice the player early. They do not ignore threats.

## Faction Tension Web (Summary)

```
Thornwood Covens ←——— hostile ———→ House Caervorn
Golvari ←————————— suspicious ——→ Gravenguard  
Compact ←—————————— competitive → Fairgean
Golvari ←————————— deep wary ———→ Fairgean

Ashen Court ←——————— war —————————→ everyone (eventually)
```

**Reconciliation quests exist for**:
- Caervorn / Thornwood (late game — requires Honored in both simultaneously, which means navigating the tension carefully)
- Golvari / Gravenguard (mid game — Commander Drest and Deep-Speaker Keth both want a resolution, neither will make the first move)
- Fairgean / surface world (early-mid game — the Fairgean's situation is urgent and they will deal with someone who proves worthy)
